# Community Code of Conduct

Sparrow welcomes thoughtful contributors of every background and experience
level. Be direct about technical disagreements while remaining respectful of
the people involved.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualised attention, doxxing, deliberate
misuse of private data, and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable in
project spaces. Respect legal rights and do not use the project community to
coordinate copyright infringement or other abuse.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the
repository owner through your existing private collaboration channel. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
